# StackFolio

StackFolio tracks precious-metal ownership and its value at metal spot prices.

## Language

**Historical Stack Value**:
The spot value of the metal actually owned on a given past date, using that date's owned quantities and the latest available metal quotes on or before that date. It changes with both ownership and spot prices; it excludes cash from sales and does not measure profit or loss.
_Avoid_: Profit/loss, investment return, acquisition cost

**Profit/loss (P/L)**:
The gain or loss relative to actual acquisition costs, accounting for applicable fees and either sale proceeds for sold holdings or a stated valuation for unsold holdings. Acquisition-day spot value alone is not actual acquisition cost.
_Avoid_: Stack value, change in stack value
